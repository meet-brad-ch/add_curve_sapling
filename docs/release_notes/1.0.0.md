Sapling Tree Gen 1.0.0 works on Blender 5.2 and later, with all new code.

- Wind animation works again (0.3.7 fails on Blender 5).
- Edit Sapling Tree: open the settings of a tree again and change them.
- Duplicate gives a whole, independent tree.
- The tree is a live Geometry Nodes object: bevel and resolution stay editable.
- Wind without bones, and an armature rig with fewer bones that exports as a skinned mesh.
- Translucent Thin Wall leaves, and blossoms (cherry, orange, magnolia).
- 29 built-in presets, 20 of them new: tree-gen species, palms, bamboo and coastal trees.
- Clumps of trunks, helix stems and bend variation.
- Trees generate up to 2 times faster.
- A setting that cannot make a tree stops with a message that names it.

Presets page: https://projects.blender.org/extensions/add_curve_sapling/src/branch/main/docs/presets.md
